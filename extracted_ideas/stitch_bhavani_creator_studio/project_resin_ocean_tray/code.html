Resin Ocean Tray

Bring the calming essence of the sea into your home. This project guides you through creating a stunning, glossy ocean wave effect on a wooden tray base.

school Difficulty Intermediate
schedule Time 4 Hours
group Creators 1.2k+

The Complete Kit

  • check_circle Premium Epoxy Resin & Hardener
  • check_circle Ocean Pigment Set (3 Colors)
  • check_circle Solid Acacia Wood Tray Base
  • check_circle Mixing Cups & Stirrers

The Process

Step 01

Mix the Base

Carefully measure and mix your resin and hardener, then separate into cups for coloring.

Step 02

Pour the Ocean

Layer your dark and light blues to create depth, blending them gently on the tray surface.

Step 03

Create the Waves

Add the white resin line and use a heat gun to push it over the blue, creating beautiful cellular lacing.
